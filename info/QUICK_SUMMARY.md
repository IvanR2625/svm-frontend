## The 8 Limitations (Now Real & Specific)

1. **Limited Sample Population Diversity**
   - 205 samples from single NCBI SRA project
   - No geographic/ethnicity/SES documentation
   - Limits generalization to other populations

2. **Cross-Sectional Data Only**
   - Single timepoint per person
   - Cannot assess temporal dynamics
   - No longitudinal follow-up

3. **Unmeasured Confounding Variables**
   - Antibiotics alter microbiome by 20-35%
   - Diet, stress, sleep, exercise, medications uncontrolled
   - Cannot isolate MDD-specific signal

4. **Correlation ≠ Causation**
   - Bidirectional gut-brain axis
   - Cannot determine causal direction
   - Cannot infer therapeutic targets

5. **Black-Box Model Interpretation**
   - SVM doesn't explain why predictions are made
   - Feature importance rankings lack biological mechanism
   - Functional validation needed

6. **16S rRNA Sequencing Bias**
   - Genus-level only (344 genera), not species-level
   - PCR bias, rare organisms missed
   - Shotgun metagenomics would be more comprehensive

7. **Model Performance Ceiling**
   - Moderate accuracy limited by noise
   - No microbiome-alone model reaches clinical-grade
   - Requires multimodal biomarker approach

8. **Lack of Clinical Metadata**
   - No MDD severity, treatment status, medication history
   - Cannot separate MDD effects from treatment effects
   - Reduces clinical relevance
