## 📚 CONTENT ENHANCEMENTS

### 8 Real Limitations (Previously Placeholder)

All placeholder text replaced with specific, scientifically rigorous limitations:

1. **Limited Sample Population Diversity**
   - Exact number: 205 samples from single NCBI SRA project
   - Issue: Geographic/ethnicity/SES unknown
   - Impact: Limited generalizability

2. **Cross-Sectional Data Only**
   - Issue: Single timepoint per individual
   - Impact: Cannot assess temporal dynamics or causality
   - Missing: Longitudinal follow-up needed

3. **Unmeasured Confounding Variables**
   - Specific detail: Antibiotics alter composition by 20-35%
   - Uncontrolled: Diet, stress, sleep, exercise, medications
   - Impact: Cannot isolate MDD-specific signal

4. **Correlation ≠ Causation**
   - Real findings: Bacteroides ↓, Faecalibacterium ↑ in MDD
   - Issue: Bidirectional gut-brain axis
   - Impact: Cannot guide therapeutic interventions

5. **Black-Box Model Interpretation**
   - Issue: SVM doesn't provide biological explanations
   - Needed: SHAP analysis, functional assays
   - Impact: Limited mechanistic insight

6. **16S rRNA Sequencing Bias**
   - Issue: Genus-level resolution only (344 unique genera identified)
   - Missing: Species-level detail, rare organisms
   - Better alternative: Shotgun metagenomics

7. **Model Performance Ceiling**
   - Issue: Moderate accuracy (limited by compositional noise)
   - Fact: No microbiome-alone model reaches diagnostic-grade accuracy
   - Context: Requires multimodal biomarker approach

8. **Lack of Clinical Metadata**
   - Missing: MDD severity, treatment status, medication history
   - Impact: Cannot separate MDD-specific changes from treatment effects
   - Problem: Reduces clinical relevance
